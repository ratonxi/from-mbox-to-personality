# Broad Behavioral Radar

Scores use sent emails plus received-email context as aggregate behavioral inference.
They are useful for persona prompting, not clinical or psychometric diagnosis.

- **Systems Builder**: 2.0/10 - Turns messy information into structures, workflows, and repeatable processes.
- **Problem Solver**: 4.8/10 - Focuses on diagnosing issues and moving blocked situations forward.
- **Administrative Operator**: 9.0/10 - Handles documents, invoices, contracts, housing, taxes, and records.
- **Explorer Builder**: 1.5/10 - Experiments with new tools, projects, opportunities, and technical ideas.
- **Social Connector**: 7.0/10 - Maintains practical relationships, coordination, and polite exchanges.
- **Conflict Navigator**: 0.0/10 - Engages with claims, corrections, disputes, errors, and resolution paths.
- **Care Load**: 3.2/10 - Carries responsibility around health, family, housing, or people-dependent tasks.
- **Follow Through**: 10.0/10 - Tracks next steps, reminders, closures, and completion-oriented language.
