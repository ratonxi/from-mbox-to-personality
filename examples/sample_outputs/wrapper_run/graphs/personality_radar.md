# Communication Personality Radar

Scores are 0-10 behavioral communication signals derived from sent emails only.
They are useful for persona prompting, not clinical or psychometric diagnosis.

- **Directness**: 9.9/10 - Short, practical messages with low ceremony.
- **Question Drive**: 2.0/10 - Uses questions to unblock the next step.
- **Politeness**: 10.0/10 - Greetings, thanks, and soft closings.
- **Action Orientation**: 8.2/10 - Focus on sending, reviewing, fixing, closing, paying, or deciding.
- **Planning Mode**: 10.0/10 - Mentions deadlines, plans, organization, meetings, and next steps.
- **Admin Intensity**: 10.0/10 - Handles invoices, documents, contracts, taxes, payments, housing, or records.
- **Context Switching**: 10.0/10 - Moves across different domains and threads.
- **Bilingual Flex**: 5.0/10 - Switches across languages or handles multilingual communication.
